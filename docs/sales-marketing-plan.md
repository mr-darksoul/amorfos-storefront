## 1. Bottom line

1. **The store works; it hasn't sold yet.** 2 real outside orders in 5 months (both a ₹599 5 Mukhi).
2. **The premium price is deliberate, but the product pages don't prove the quality.** Visitors see an
   Amazon-style title, a "46% OFF" badge and a photo like a ₹300 seller's. First hour of tracking:
   17 ad visits, 28 s average, no add-to-carts.
3. **No reason to buy today.** No COD, no first-order offer, no bundles, no gifting.
4. **One channel.** Google Search only; Meta blocked; Instagram 1 post; Journal silent since 5 Aug;
   8 email sign-ups; WhatsApp messages off in production.
5. **The job is to prove the grade, not cut price** — then add COD and an offer so a convinced visitor can buy.

## 3. Gaps and fixes

### A. Quality proof (biggest gap)
| Gap | Fix |
|---|---|
| "Higher grade" isn't defined | Write the **Amorfos Grade**: minimum size per mukhi, clear natural mukhi lines, no cracks/holes/filler, natural (not carved or joined), silver purity + weight, what the certificate covers |
| Product pages don't show it | **Proof block** per product: mukhi close-up, bead next to a coin with exact mm, silver stamp + grams, that bead's certificate, one side-by-side vs a typical market bead (no competitor named) |
| No video | 10–15 s per hero product: turn the bead, show the lines, open the sealed pouch |
| 14 Mukhi (₹16,499): "approx. varies", 0 reviews | Exact size + certificate; until then, ads go to a WhatsApp consult |
| No person behind the brand | Founder photo + note; selection/packing table photos |

### B. Price presentation
| Gap | Fix |
|---|---|
| Permanent "46% OFF ₹1,100" reads as a market stall | One honest price; real offers only (first order, bundles, festivals) |
| Amazon-style titles | "6 Mukhi Rudraksha · Nepal · Silver Pendant" on page; keywords stay in the meta title |
| Size mismatches (e.g. ~18 mm vs 18–22 mm) | One exact size per product |

### C. Offer and order value
| Gap | Fix |
|---|---|
| No COD | Razorpay Magic Checkout or Shiprocket Checkout; ₹49 fee, cap ~₹3,000, WhatsApp confirm before shipping |
| No first-order offer | 10% off or free shipping on first order — replaces the PDF in the popup |
| Order value ~₹678 | 5 + 7 Mukhi pair, pendant + mala, family set; "complete the pair" in cart under ₹999 |
| No gifting | Gift wrap, gift note, "Gift a blessing" collection |

### D. Reviews
| Gap | Fix |
|---|---|
| Old Amazon reviews on product pages with "VERIFIED" describe older stock | Off product pages; brand-level proof only: **"5,000+ beads delivered since 2018"** |
| No D2C reviews | Request 3 days after delivery, photo upload, small thank-you coupon |

### E. Homepage and choosing
| Gap | Fix |
|---|---|
| Hero and "Bestsellers" open with ₹19,499 13 Mukhi + Kaal Sarp Dosh | Lead with 5 and 7 Mukhi |
| Nothing answers "which mukhi is for me?" | Shop by intention, by rashi, 3-question bead finder → product or WhatsApp consult |
| English only | Hindi top-5 product pages + Hindi bead guide; Hinglish ad copy |
| Every PDP cross-sells the ₹19,499 bead | Similar-price items or the bundle partner |

### F. Paid ads
| Gap | Fix |
|---|---|
| "…price" keywords bring bargain hunters | Lower/pause; push "original", "certified", "Nepali", "silver pendant", "best quality", "real" |
| Grade not in the ads | Headlines like "20 mm+ Nepal bead · Lab Certified · Solid silver" |
| ₹5 manual bids likely throttling spend | Watch 48 h; if low, Maximize clicks with ₹8–10 cap on 5/7 Mukhi |
| High-value beads sold one-click | 12–14 Mukhi + Gauri Shankar → WhatsApp consult; WhatsApp click = conversion |
| No break-even | Unit cost per SKU → target ad cost per order |
| Meta blocked | Fix account → proof reels, founder video, retargeting |

### G. Channels
1. **Astrologer & pandit affiliates (#1 to build):** personal codes, 10–15% commission, printed grade card, "recommended by" at checkout.
2. **WhatsApp as the sales counter:** turn on in prod; confirmations, shipping updates, consults, broadcasts.
3. **YouTube Shorts:** "how to tell a real rudraksha", grade close-ups, unboxing.
4. **Instagram:** queue 12 posts before any Meta spend.
5. **B2B:** bulk prices for puja stores and pandits.

## 5. Budget guardrails
- One monthly ad cap across Google + Meta — suggested ₹15–20k in December while conversion is fixed.
- Break-even ad cost per order = order value − product cost − silver & packing − shipping − ~2% payment fee (− returned-COD losses).
- Scale only when PDP → add to cart ≥ 5% and ad cost per order < break-even for 2 weeks running.
